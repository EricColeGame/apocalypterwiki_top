### Gathering Gusmanak's Staging Supplies

Before entering the operational theater, talk directly to Gusmanak in the staging area. He tasks you with securing three distinct survival provisions scattered around the staging grounds:

1. **Canned Beans:** Located inside the residential house situated directly to the left of Gusmanak's post.
2. **Medkit:** Positioned inside the primary green tent pitched behind the prominent blue commercial building.
3. **Bloxy Cola:** Resting atop the sacrificial altar inside the dungeon chamber, accessed by vaulting up the yellow construction blocks adjacent to the "HOPE" billboard.

Confirm receipt of all three provisions with Gusmanak to activate your outbound teleportation sequence.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Earning the Apocalypse Rising 2 Survivor Badge

Once your boots touch the ground on the starter island, open your main interface and inspect the active **Event** tab. The progression system requires three simultaneous objectives completed within a single character life. If another player snipes you or an infected mob overwhelms you, your checklist resets back to baseline.

```
[Start Run] ➔ [Find 3 Core Items] ➔ [Eliminate 5 Infected] ➔ [Walk Studs + Observe Horizon] ➔ [Badge Awarded]
```

To earn your **apocalypse rising 2 survivor badge**, you must carefully execute three synchronized survival trials:

### 1. Find The Loot (Trio Requirement)
You must find and simultaneously equip three specific equipment classes: a primary firearm, any class of backpack, and a dedicated melee weapon. Finding them on the floor is insufficient; all three must occupy active loadout slots at the same time. 

Melee weapons routinely serve as the primary bottleneck during early runs. Scour high-density industrial and maritime zones rather than residential closets. The Starter Island Junkyard, the Military Prison compound, and the rusted Destroyer Shipwreck provide the most reliable drop rates for crowbars, knives, and utility tools.

### 2. Zombie Hunter
Eliminate five infected targets using either stealth melee strikes or your newly acquired firearm. Conserve your ammunition whenever possible; firing unsuppressed weapons in open terrain draws unwanted attention from roaming bandit squads.

### 3. Explore & Survive
Log the required travel distance (measured in studs via your Event tracker) while surviving long enough to observe at least one full sunrise or sunset cycle. You can naturally complete the distance quota while running between high-tier loot spawns.

| Event Objective | Success Condition | Recommended Spawn Locations | Tactical Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Find The Loot** | Equip primary weapon, backpack, and melee tool | Destroyer Shipwreck, Junkyard, Military Prison | Secure the melee weapon first to avoid inventory clutter |
| **Zombie Hunter** | Destroy 5 infected hosts | Outlying farmsteads, road barriers | Aim for headshots with silenced sidearms |
| **Explore & Survive** | Cover required stud distance + view sunrise/sunset | Coastal perimeter roads, open rail tracks | Keep hydration above 75% to maintain sprint stamina |

Completing these tasks directly triggers your **apocalypse rising 2 survivor badge** unlock, registering your baseline victory on your profile and granting the foundational Biting BLOXikin hub reward.

## Transitioning to the Secret Quest: Locating the Hidden NPC

Unlocking the **apocalypse rising 2 survivor badge** is merely the opening chapter for completionists. Once your badge confirms in the system, a hidden secondary track opens on the live server map. This quest is administered by an unmarked NPC isolated in the deep wilderness.

You will not find navigation pings, mini-map breadcrumbs, or compass markers pointing toward this contact. Reaching the operative requires a sustained cross-country march across open fields, hay bale pastures, and undulating terrain far removed from normal starter zones.

| Travel Factor | Daylight Operations | Nighttime Infiltration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Visual Navigation** | High landmark clarity; easy water tower spotting | Low visibility; high risk of stumbling into infected packs |
| **Hostile Player Risk** | Severe; open fields make you vulnerable to snipers | Minimal; silhouette blending conceals movement |
| **Zombies Aggro Range** | Standard line-of-sight detection | Reduced alert radius; safer sprint lanes |
| **Recommended Strategy** | Move via woodlines and rolling dips | Stick strictly to marked dirt road shoulders |

Player experience suggests executing this journey during twilight or dusk. The low sun lowers the profile of your movement against the horizon while leaving sufficient light to recognize agricultural landmarks.

## Complete Scavenger Hunt Item Locations and Collection Order

When you interact with the hidden contact, they issue an exacting scavenger hunt. You must collect the items in the precise sequence mandated by the dialogue prompts; picking them up out of order can invalidate the turn-in sequence.

The operational route spans multiple rural hamlets, industrial outbuildings, and domestic residences. Pack light: carry a reliable sidearm, two medical dressings, and leave ample backpack space open for quest items.

| Item Sequence | Designated Pickup Item | Target Structure / Architectural Marker | Exact Placement Clue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1** | Binoculars | Blue-trimmed concrete utility station | Ground floor near red wall graffiti |
| **Step 2** | Purple College Backpack | Adjacent beige single-family home | Center floorboards in main living area |
| **Step 3** | M1 Carbine 15rd Magazine | Weathered brown timber shed | Tucked into the interior rear-right corner |
| **Step 4** | Brown Briefcase | Two-story residential farmhouse | Resting beneath a timber work table |
| **Step 5** | MAC-10 Magazine | Same farmhouse workshop | Directly beside the briefcase on the tabletop |
| **Step 6** | 9mm Parabellum Ammunition | Same farmhouse workshop | Stacked adjacent to the MAC-10 magazine |

### Navigating the Graffiti Landmark

The most critical landmark along this route is the reinforced concrete utility structure detailed with contrasting blue trim. Upon breaching the entryway, look across the back wall for the crimson spray-painted message reading **THERE IS NO ESCAPE**. 

Pick up the Binoculars resting on the floorboards directly beneath the paint. From there, immediately clear the adjoining beige residential building to collect the Purple College Backpack. Because these two objectives sit immediately next to one another, securing the opening third of the hunt requires very little cross-country travel.

After completing the graffiti cluster, pivot your heading toward the distant rural water tower. Sweep through the outlying wooden sheds to secure your M1 Carbine magazine, then systematically breach the main workshop house to loot the briefcase and accompanying firearm accessories from the workbench.

## Turning in Items, Sync Delays, and the Road to Year Infinity

Once all six manifest items rest inside your inventory, backtrack carefully to the hidden NPC. Avoid main highways and known vehicle spawn hubs during your return leg. 

When presenting your findings to the NPC:
- Interact cleanly without opening concurrent inventory screens.
- Allow dialogue windows to completely print out before advancing.
- Do not immediately disconnect or exit the server upon completion.

Server desynchronization represents the most common issue reported during progression runs. The secret flag and fragment award often take 30 to 90 seconds to write back to the master database. If you disconnect prematurely, you may find your inventory cleared while the quest marker resets.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I earn the apocalypse rising 2 survivor badge inside private VIP servers?
Yes, players can fulfill the event criteria inside private servers provided event mechanics remain enabled by server settings. Completing the objectives in a private environment substantially reduces the danger of aggressive player-versus-player ambushes while collecting your required weapons and stud travel counts.

### What happens if I die while working toward the apocalypse rising 2 survivor badge?
If you die during the initial survival run, all current progression points across the three core objectives reset to zero. You must respawn, locate a new primary firearm, melee weapon, and backpack, eliminate five new infected targets, and complete the movement distance criteria entirely within that single fresh lifespan.

### Why won't the secret NPC accept my scavenger hunt items?
If the wilderness contact refuses to accept your deliveries, check your inventory order. The NPC requires items to be collected and submitted in the explicit order specified during dialogue. Additionally, verify that your account has claimed all preceding event hub year rewards and that the **apocalypse rising 2 survivor badge** is officially registered to your profile.

### Do I need the secret badge to keep the basic survivor badge?
No. The **apocalypse rising 2 survivor badge** is an independent achievement earned by completing the three basic tasks on the Event tab. The secret NPC quest line is an optional, high-difficulty extension intended for players hunting down all twenty universal fragments for the endgame Dominus challenge.
