## How to Find Reliable Squadmates and Survive Longer

## Reporting Exploiters, Bugs, and Submitting Feedback

No open-world survival game is completely immune to bad actors and unexpected software glitches. Fortunately, community moderation inside the **apocalypse rising 2 discord** remains proactive. The moderation team maintains structured workflows so players can report script abusers, map glitches, and game-breaking performance bugs effectively.

To ensure your ticket receives prompt attention, avoid posting emotional rants in general chat channels. Moderators evaluate claims based on objective evidence rather than text accusations. Player experience shows that tickets containing clear video links and specific server details are processed significantly faster than vague submissions.

To keep discussions constructive and prevent malicious raids, the server enforces strict onboarding and behavioral policies. New members must complete standard verification steps before unlocking posting privileges in recruitment and discussion channels.

- **No Toxicity or Harassment:** Trash talk is part of survival combat, but targeted harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks result in swift administrative mutes or bans.

- **Keep Media in Designated Channels:** Avoid spamming gameplay clips, memes, or recruitment ads in general conversation threads. Use the designated media and recruitment boards.
